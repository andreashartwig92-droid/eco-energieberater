# Kooperationsmodell: Manual J/S/D für US-Bauträger

*Vorbemerkung auf Deutsch, Vertragstext auf Englisch. Muster – vor Unterzeichnung von einem im Zielstaat zugelassenen Anwalt und einem Steuerberater (Deutschland und USA) prüfen lassen. Eckige Klammern [ ] sind auszufüllen bzw. auszuwählen.*

## Wie die Kooperation funktioniert

Der Energieberater („Designer") erstellt die Lastberechnung (ACCA Manual J), die Geräteauswahl (Manual S) und das Kanalnetz (Manual D) mit ACCA-anerkannter Software. Ein US-Partner („Partner") mit Lizenz und Versicherung im Zielstaat hält den Vertrag mit dem Bauträger, prüft die Unterlagen, unterschreibt die Einreichung und tritt gegenüber der Bauaufsicht auf. Der Bauträger sieht einen Anbieter, dahinter arbeiten zwei selbstständige Unternehmen.

| Partnertyp | Vorteil | Zu beachten |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lizenzierter HVAC- / Mechanical Contractor | Reicht Bauanträge ohnehin ein, hat die Bauträgerkontakte | Darf nur im eigenen Lizenzstaat einreichen |
| HERS-Rating-Firma | Bedient Bauträger bereits mit Energienachweisen, Manual J/S/D ergänzt das Paket | Prüft, ob die Bauaufsicht Rater-Unterschrift akzeptiert |
| Ingenieurbüro mit Professional Engineer | Kann in Gemeinden mit Siegelpflicht siegeln | PE muss die Arbeit wirklich prüfen und verantworten („plan stamping" ist verboten) |

Preislogik (Richtwerte 2026): Der Partner verkauft das Paket für 350 bis 500 US-Dollar je Grundriss an den Bauträger. Der Designer erhält einen festen Großhandelspreis von [150 bis 250] US-Dollar je Grundriss oder [60 bis 70] % des Nettoerlöses. Weitere Ausrichtungen desselben Grundrisses und Revisionen werden gesondert bepreist.

Was der Vertrag absichert: klare Verantwortung für die Einreichung, keine Haftung des Designers gegenüber dem Bauträger, Softwarelizenz auf den tatsächlichen Bearbeiter, Lieferzeiten, Datenschutz für Pläne, Kundenschutz in beide Richtungen, Steuerformalitäten für den ausländischen Dienstleister.

## Recitals

The Designer prepares room-by-room heating and cooling load calculations, equipment selection and duct design for residential buildings using software recognized by the Air Conditioning Contractors of America (ACCA). The Partner holds the licenses, insurance and local presence required to contract with home builders and to submit permit documents to building departments in [state(s)]. The Parties wish to offer home builders a combined "HVAC Design Package" while remaining independent businesses.

## Section 1 – Scope of Cooperation

1. The Partner markets and sells HVAC Design Packages to home builders, developers and HVAC contractors (the "Clients") in its own name and on its own account.
2. For each order the Designer prepares, as a subcontractor of the Partner:
   a) Manual J, 8th edition, room-by-room load calculation,
   b) Manual S equipment selection, including heat pump balance point where applicable,
   c) Manual D duct design with friction rate worksheet and duct layout,
   d) on request, an energy code compliance input package (IECC [2021] R405 or prescriptive path) for a HERS Rater,
   together the "Deliverables".
3. The Deliverables are produced with ACCA-approved software ([Wrightsoft Right-Suite Universal / Elite RHVAC], version [ ]), licensed to the Designer, and are delivered as PDF reports plus the native software file.
4. This Agreement creates no obligation to place or accept a minimum number of orders. Exclusivity applies only as set out in Section 9.

## Section 2 – Roles and Responsibilities

1. **Partner:** acquisition of Clients, contract with the Client, collection of project data (plans, specifications, orientation, site, climate zone, equipment preferences), technical review of the Deliverables, signature and submission to the authority having jurisdiction ("AHJ"), communication with the AHJ and the Client, invoicing of the Client, compliance with state licensing and insurance requirements.
2. **Designer:** preparation of the Deliverables according to ACCA Manuals J, S and D and the data provided, documentation of assumptions, revisions under Section 5, upkeep of software licenses and ACCA/RESNET training.
3. The Partner is the party responsible toward the Client and the AHJ. The Partner reviews each Deliverable before submission and confirms by its signature that it has verified the inputs and results. Where the AHJ requires the seal of a licensed Professional Engineer, the Partner shall provide it only through an engineer who has reviewed the work and is in responsible charge of it.
4. The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture or agency relationship. Neither Party may bind the other.

## Section 3 – Project Workflow

1. The Partner submits an order with the data listed in Exhibit A (project intake form). Incomplete orders are returned with a list of missing items; the delivery period starts when the order is complete.
2. Standard delivery period: [5] business days from complete order. Rush delivery ([48] hours): surcharge under Exhibit B.
3. The Designer delivers through [shared folder / project portal]. The Partner confirms receipt and either approves the Deliverables or requests a revision within [5] business days.
4. The Designer is available for AHJ comments on the Deliverables for [90] days after delivery at no extra charge, provided the comments concern the Deliverables as ordered.

## Section 4 – Fees and Payment

*Option A – wholesale price per plan:*
1. The Partner pays the Designer the fees in Exhibit B (currently [ ] USD per HVAC Design Package up to [2,000] sq ft conditioned floor area, [ ] USD per sq ft above, [ ] USD per additional orientation of the same plan).

*Option B – revenue share:*
1. The Partner pays the Designer [ ] % of the net fee invoiced to and collected from the Client for the Deliverables, but not less than [ ] USD per plan.

2. Fees are exclusive of any sales or use tax. The Parties assume that design services provided from abroad are not subject to US sales tax in [state]; the Partner shall inform the Designer of any contrary assessment.
3. The Designer invoices monthly in USD. Payment within [30] days by [ACH / wire / Wise] without deduction of transfer fees. Late payments bear interest at [1] % per month.
4. The Designer provides IRS Form W-8BEN-E (or W-8BEN) to the Partner. The Parties assume that no US withholding tax applies to the fees; each Party is responsible for its own taxes in its own country.

## Section 5 – Revisions and Changes

1. One revision round per plan caused by the Designer's error, or by AHJ comments on the Deliverables as ordered, is included.
2. Revisions caused by changed plans, changed equipment, changed orientation or new Client requests are charged under Exhibit B.
3. Each revision is delivered as a new report version with a change note.

## Section 6 – Quality, Standards and Software

1. The Deliverables follow ACCA Manual J (8th ed.), Manual S and Manual D in the editions referenced by the code adopted in the project jurisdiction, with project-specific inputs (no block loads for permit purposes unless the AHJ accepts them).
2. The Designer uses only data provided by the Partner or published design data (ACCA/ASHRAE climate data, manufacturer expanded performance data). Assumptions are listed in the report.
3. Software outputs carry the name of the licensed Designer as preparer; the Partner is named as submitting party. The Partner shall not alter the reports except for its signature block and cover sheet.
4. The Designer maintains its ACCA/RESNET training and software maintenance at its own cost and provides evidence on request.

## Section 7 – Liability and Insurance

1. The Designer is liable to the Partner for Deliverables that do not meet Section 6, limited to re-performance and, if re-performance fails, to the fee for the affected plan. Liability for consequential damages, lost profits or equipment replacement is excluded except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.
2. The Partner alone is liable toward the Client and the AHJ and shall indemnify the Designer against third-party claims arising from the Partner's submission, installation or Client relationship, except to the extent caused by the Designer's gross negligence.
3. The Partner maintains general liability and, where available, professional liability (errors and omissions) insurance of at least [ ] USD per occurrence and names the Designer as additional insured on request. The Designer maintains professional liability insurance covering foreign work of at least [ ] EUR/USD if available on reasonable terms.

## Section 8 – Confidentiality and Data

1. Plans, specifications, Client lists and prices are confidential and may be used only for this cooperation. The obligation survives termination by [3] years.
2. The Designer may store project data on servers in [Germany / EU] and process it under the GDPR; the Partner confirms that the Client has consented to the transfer where required.
3. Each Party may use anonymized project data for internal statistics and software validation.

## Section 9 – Clients, Non-Solicitation, Exclusivity

1. Clients acquired by the Partner remain the Partner's Clients. The Designer shall not offer HVAC design services directly to a Client introduced by the Partner during the term and for [12] months thereafter without the Partner's consent.
2. The Partner shall not commission a third party to copy, reverse engineer or re-brand the Designer's reports and shall not solicit the Designer's employees.
3. [Option: Territorial exclusivity – the Designer works exclusively with the Partner in [state(s)] while the Partner places at least [ ] orders per quarter; otherwise exclusivity ends automatically.]

## Section 10 – Marketing

1. The Partner may describe the Deliverables as prepared "in cooperation with [Designer]" and may use the Designer's name, logo and credentials with prior written consent, revocable at any time.
2. The Designer may list the Partner as a reference with prior written consent.
3. Neither Party shall make statements suggesting that the Designer is licensed in [state] or that the Designer submits documents to the AHJ.

## Section 11 – Term and Termination

1. This Agreement starts on [date] and runs for [12] months, renewing automatically for [12]-month periods unless terminated with [3] months' notice to the end of a period.
2. Termination for cause with immediate effect remains unaffected, in particular on loss of the Partner's license or insurance, repeated late payment, or breach of Sections 6, 8 or 9.
3. Orders placed before termination are completed and paid under this Agreement.

## Section 12 – Governing Law and Disputes

1. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [ ], excluding its conflict-of-law rules and the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods.
2. The Parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes amicably within [30] days. Thereafter disputes are resolved by [binding arbitration under the Commercial Arbitration Rules of the American Arbitration Association, seat [city, state], one arbitrator, English language] / [the courts of [county, state]].
3. Amendments require written form (e-mail suffices). If a provision is invalid, the remainder stays in force.
