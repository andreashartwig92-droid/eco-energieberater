How it works

You send plans, we send a signed report. The whole exchange runs by upload and email, nationwide, in any IECC climate zone.

Step 1 · Day 0

Upload the plans

Floor plans, elevations, window schedule, insulation values, orientation, site address and the name of the building department. Our intake form lists every item.

Step 2 · Day 1–3

We design in Wrightsoft

Room-by-room Manual J with local design conditions, Manual S selection from manufacturer expanded data, Manual D duct layout with friction-rate worksheet.

Step 3 · Day 3

You submit, we back you up

You receive the PDF package and the native file. Your licensed contractor or builder submits. If the AHJ comments on our work, we revise at no charge for 90 days.

Questions

Short answers to the things contractors ask before their first pack.

Who signs and submits the report?

We prepare the calculations and sign as preparer. Your licensed HVAC contractor, builder or engineer submits them to the building department and remains the responsible party. We do not file permits and we do not provide a Professional Engineer seal. If your jurisdiction requires a seal, tell us and we will say so before you order.

Which states do you cover?

All 50 states. Design conditions for over 1,500 US locations are built into our software, and we keep a register of code editions and submittal rules by jurisdiction. California Title 24 CF1R forms are not part of a pack; Manual J for California projects is.

What software do you use?

Wrightsoft Right-Suite Universal, ACCA-approved for Manual J, S and D. Reports carry the software certification, and you receive the native file so any designer can open and edit it later.

What counts as one system?

One piece of equipment serving one zone in one dwelling: a furnace with coil, a heat pump with air handler, a ductless multi-zone head group on one outdoor unit. A house with an upstairs and downstairs system uses two systems from your pack.

How fast is turnaround?

Three business days from a complete intake for Pack 10, two for Packs 20 and 30. Rush delivery in 48 hours is $50. The clock starts when every item on the intake list is in.

What if the building department rejects it?

If the comment concerns our work, we revise free of charge within 90 days of delivery. If the comment results from changed plans or equipment, the revision is $40 per system.

Can I buy just one calc?

No. Packs start at 10 systems. That is how we keep the price at $100 and the turnaround predictable. If you only need one calc a year, a per-job provider is the better fit.

Do you design hydronic or heat pump retrofits?

Yes. Our designers come from hydronic heating and heat pump retrofit work in Europe. Balance point, backup heat, cold-climate heat pumps and existing radiator checks are included when the project calls for them.
